# Leave Policy

*Larkspur & Finch Ltd. is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.*

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Policy owner: People Team.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive **25 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours. After **5 years** of continuous service, the allowance rises to **28 days**.

Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**, after which they expire. Leave requests of 3 days or more must be submitted in the HR portal at least **14 days** in advance.

## Sick leave

Employees receive **10 days** of fully paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for any absence longer than **3 consecutive working days**. Unused sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives **20 weeks** of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives **6 weeks** of fully paid leave, which must be taken within **12 months** of the birth or adoption. Employees become eligible after **6 months** of service.

## Other leave

- Bereavement leave: **5 days** paid for an immediate family member, **2 days** for other relatives.
- Volunteering leave: **2 days** paid per year for registered charities.
- Jury duty: paid in full for its duration.

Questions about leave should be sent to the People Team at people@larkspurfinch.example.
