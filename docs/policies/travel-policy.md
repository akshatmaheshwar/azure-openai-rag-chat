# Travel Policy

*Larkspur & Finch Ltd. is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.*

Effective date: 1 February 2026. Policy owner: Finance Team.

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through the company travel portal at least **14 days** before departure where possible. Trips must be approved by your line manager before booking. International trips also need department head approval.

## Flights

Economy class is standard for flights under **6 hours**. Premium economy may be booked for flights of **6 hours or longer**. Business class is permitted only for flights over **10 hours** and requires Finance Director approval. Checked baggage is covered for trips of **3 nights** or more.

## Rail

Standard class is used for rail travel. First class is permitted only for journeys longer than **3 hours** where it costs less than **£50** more than standard class.

## Hotels

Nightly hotel rates (excluding breakfast) are capped at:

- **£180** in London
- **£130** elsewhere in the UK
- **£220** in New York and Tokyo

Hotels must be booked through the travel portal so the company can locate travellers in an emergency.

## Per diem

When travelling overnight, a per diem of **£50 per day** covers meals and incidentals. It replaces, and cannot be combined with, the daily meal limit in the Expenses Policy.

## Safety

Travel to countries rated high risk by the company's security provider requires approval from the IT Security Team and the Chief Operating Officer. The 24-hour travel assistance line is available to all travellers on **+44 20 7946 0000**.

Expenses for travel are claimed under the Expenses Policy within **30 days** of returning.
