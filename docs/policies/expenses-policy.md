# Expenses Policy

*Larkspur & Finch Ltd. is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.*

Effective date: 1 February 2026. Policy owner: Finance Team.

## Submitting claims

All business expenses must be submitted in the expenses app within **30 days** of the purchase date. Claims older than **90 days** will not be reimbursed. Every claim over **£25** needs an itemised receipt. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll, which runs on the **25th** of each month.

## Approval limits

- Up to **£500**: approved by your line manager.
- **£500 to £2,000**: approved by your department head.
- Over **£2,000**: requires pre-approval from the Finance Director before purchase.

## Meals and entertainment

When working away from your home office, meals are reimbursed up to **£40 per day**. Client entertainment is capped at **£75 per person**, and the names of all attendees must be listed on the claim. Alcohol is reimbursed only as part of client entertainment.

## Equipment and subscriptions

Personal software subscriptions must be approved by IT before purchase. Home office equipment is covered by a one-off allowance of **£300**, available after completing probation.

## Mileage

Use of a personal car for business travel is reimbursed at **£0.45 per mile** for the first **10,000 miles** in a tax year and **£0.25 per mile** after that. Commuting between home and your normal office is not reimbursable.

## Non-reimbursable items

Fines, personal travel upgrades, gym memberships, and lost-property replacement are never reimbursed. Misuse of the expenses process is treated as a disciplinary matter.

Questions should be sent to finance@larkspurfinch.example.
