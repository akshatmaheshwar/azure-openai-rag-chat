# Onboarding Policy

*Larkspur & Finch Ltd. is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.*

Effective date: 1 April 2026. Policy owner: People Team.

## Before day one

Offer letters are sent within **3 working days** of a verbal offer. New starters receive their laptop by courier at least **2 working days** before their start date, along with sign-in instructions. Right-to-work checks must be completed before the first day.

## First week

New starters always begin on a **Monday**. Day one includes a welcome session at **09:30**, an IT setup session, and lunch with the team. Each new starter is assigned an **onboarding buddy** from a different team, who meets them at least once a week for the first **8 weeks**.

## Mandatory training

The following must be completed within the first **14 days**:

- Security awareness training (see the IT Security Policy)
- Data protection training
- Code of conduct acknowledgement

## Probation

The probation period is **6 months**. A check-in with the line manager takes place at **1 month** and **3 months**, and a formal review at **6 months**. During probation, the notice period is **1 week** on either side. After probation, it increases to **1 month**. Probation may be extended once, by up to **3 months**.

## Benefits enrolment

Employees must enrol in the pension and health insurance schemes within **30 days** of starting. The company pension contribution is **6%** of base salary, matched against a minimum employee contribution of **4%**. Benefits not chosen in this window can be changed only at the annual enrolment in **November**.

Questions should be sent to onboarding@larkspurfinch.example.
