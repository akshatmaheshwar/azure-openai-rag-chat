# Remote Work Policy

*Larkspur & Finch Ltd. is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.*

Effective date: 1 March 2026. Policy owner: People Team.

## Hybrid working

Larkspur & Finch operates a hybrid model. Employees are expected in the office on **2 anchor days** per week, which are **Tuesday and Thursday** for most teams. The remaining days may be worked from home with manager agreement.

Core collaboration hours are **10:00 to 15:00** local time. Outside those hours, employees may arrange their working day flexibly as long as they meet their contracted hours.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to **20 working days** in any rolling 12-month period. Requests must be submitted to the People Team at least **4 weeks** in advance so that tax and immigration checks can be completed. Working from sanctioned countries is never permitted.

## Fully remote roles

A role can be designated fully remote only with approval from the department head and the People Team. Fully remote employees must still attend the company all-hands, held in person **twice a year** in **April and October**. Travel for these events is paid under the Travel Policy.

## Home setup

Employees working from home must have a safe workspace and an internet connection of at least **50 Mbps** download. The home office allowance described in the Expenses Policy (**£300**) can be used for a desk, chair, or monitor. A **£20 per month** internet allowance is paid to employees who work from home **3 or more days** per week.

## Security

Company devices must only be used on secured networks. Public Wi-Fi requires the company VPN, as set out in the IT Security Policy.
